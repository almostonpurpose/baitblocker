# Privacy policy

The policy itself lives in [`site/privacy.html`](site/privacy.html), which is the version
published at the URL given to the Chrome Web Store. It is kept in one place deliberately:
a second copy in this file would drift out of step with the published one.

Short version: the extension makes no network requests and stores your settings and a
counter in your own browser. The website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and makes no
third-party requests. The published policy covers the UK and EU GDPR requirements in full,
including the server-log processing that hosting unavoidably involves.

The named controller is Dr. Amr Aswad, as the UK and EU GDPR require. Update the version
number and date at the top of `site/privacy.html` whenever the policy changes.
