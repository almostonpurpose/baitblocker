The extension

\n\n

What it reads

\n

To mark persuasion patterns, BaitBlocker reads the visible text of pages you open. That\n reading happens inside the page, in your browser. The text is never transmitted, never\n written to disk, and never seen by us or anyone else. When you close a tab, the\n extension's record of that page is gone.

\n

The extension makes no network requests of any kind. The rules, the\n explanations and the fonts are all bundled inside the installed package. There is no\n server to send anything to.

\n\n

What it stores, and where

\n
    \n
  • Your settings — which categories are switched on, the sensitivity\n level, and the list of sites you have paused. Held in your browser's extension storage.
    • \n
  • A running tally — how many pages have been scanned and how many\n patterns were marked, split by category. Counters only: no URLs, no page text, no page\n titles, no timestamps of individual visits.
    • \n
\n

Both live in your browser and are readable only by this extension. If you have your\n browser's own sync feature switched on, your browser may replicate your settings between\n your devices using your browser vendor's infrastructure. That is processing carried out\n by your browser vendor under your own account and their privacy policy, not by us — we\n receive nothing from it. Uninstalling the extension removes all of it.

\n\n

What this means for your rights

\n

We hold no personal data from the extension. There is no account, no identifier, no\n profile and no server-side record. A request to access, correct, export or erase\n extension data has nothing for us to act on, because we have never received any. You can\n see all of it yourself in the extension's Settings page, and delete all of it by removing\n the extension.

This website
